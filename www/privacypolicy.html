Questline privacy

Questline is a personal app that runs entirely on your phone. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

Health data

With your permission, Questline reads steps, sleep, weight, heart rate and resting heart rate from Health Connect. It uses them only to show your progress and to fill in your step habit. The data is stored on this phone, is never sent anywhere, and is never shared or sold.

You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Calendar and camera

Calendar access is used only to add your Questline dates to your phone calendar and show your events. The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Nothing leaves the phone.
